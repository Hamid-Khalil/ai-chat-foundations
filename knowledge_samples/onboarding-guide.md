# Apex Dynamics Contractor and New Hire Onboarding Guide

## 1. Welcoming External Contractors (Tier 2 Engagement)
External contractors operating under Tier 2 agreements must follow distinct governance procedures compared to core full-time personnel:
- Contractor leave notice: Term contractors must provide at least 10 business days prior written notice to Lead Engineer Elena Vance before taking planned leave.
- Leave carryover: Unused contractor leave is strictly forfeited at contract termination and cannot be rolled over.
- Hardware stipend: Term contractors receive a one-time remote hardware stipend of 450 EUR (compared to the 1200 EUR standard core employee workstation setup grant).
- Physical access: Contractor security badges are yellow and expire automatically every 60 calendar days unless re-certified by their sponsoring team lead.

## 2. First-Week Operational Checkpoints
All incoming engineers must complete mandatory setup milestones within designated operational windows:
- Mandatory Security and Data Hygiene Training must be completed within 72 hours of initial credential provisioning.
- Every contractor is assigned a designated technical mentor, Marcus Brody, for an initial 14-day interlock period.
- Physical keycard replacement incurs a standardized 35 EUR administrative surcharge.
- Production deploys are strictly prohibited during an engineer's first 10 business days on the team. All changes must deploy to staging cluster `stg-node-04`.

## 3. Communication Norms and Core Tooling
Personnel must configure notifications across standard internal communications channels:
- All engineers must join `#announce-all`, `#eng-guild`, and `#daily-standup`.
- Standup updates must be posted asynchronously by 09:15 local time Monday through Friday.
- Technical queries relating to infrastructure access should be routed to internal node 0x4D2.
